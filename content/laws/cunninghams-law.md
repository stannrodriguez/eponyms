## Origin

Ward Cunningham built the first wiki in 1995, which is why the line is attached to him. He did not coin it — Steven McGeady named it after him, recalling advice Cunningham gave him in the early 1980s, and Cunningham has since said the attribution garbles what he actually said, which was about Usenet rather than the internet at large.

## Mechanism

Answering a question is unpaid work with an uncertain audience. Correcting a visible error is cheap, has a target already written, and pays in status — the corrector gets to demonstrate expertise against a fixed reference point. A wrong answer converts an open-ended request into a concrete claim, and concrete claims attract responses that questions do not.
