## Origin

Roy Amara ran the Institute for the Future, the Palo Alto forecasting group spun out of RAND, from 1971 to 1990. He stated the line in conversation and in talks rather than in a paper, and it circulated among forecasters for years before it was written down and named.

## Mechanism

The two halves have different causes. In the short run, the demo is the cheap part: a working capability still needs infrastructure, regulation, trained users and rebuilt workflows, and those move on the timescale of institutions rather than of engineering. In the long run, the same technology becomes a component other people build on, and the recombinations compound — which is a curve people forecast badly because they extrapolate from the rate they can currently see.
