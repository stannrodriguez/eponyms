## Origin

Douglas Hofstadter stated it in Gödel, Escher, Bach in 1979, in a passage about how badly early researchers had predicted when a computer would beat a human at chess. The self-reference is the point: the correction clause is included so that the law survives its own application.

## Mechanism

Estimates are built by decomposing the job into the steps you can currently name, and the steps you cannot name are exactly the ones that will cost the most. Delays are also correlated rather than independent — the thing that blocks one step usually blocks the next — so slippage compounds instead of averaging out. The recursive clause holds because applying a multiplier does not add the missing steps; it scales the same incomplete decomposition.
