## Origin

Laurence J. Peter was a professor of education who wrote the book with the playwright Raymond Hull; The Peter Principle appeared in 1969 and sold in the millions. It is written as satire — mock case studies, invented jargon — but the central claim is empirical and Peter meant it.

## Mechanism

Promotion is a reward for performance in the current job, and it is paid in a different job. The two jobs need different skills: the best engineer's advantage does not transfer to managing engineers. Someone who performs well keeps being moved until they reach a role they perform badly, at which point the promotions stop and they stay. A 2018 study of American sales organizations found exactly this pattern — sales performance strongly predicted promotion to management and predicted worse performance once there.
