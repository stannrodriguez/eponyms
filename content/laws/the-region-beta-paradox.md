## Origin

Daniel Gilbert, Timothy Wilson and colleagues named it in a 2004 Psychological Science paper, The Peculiar Longevity of Things Not So Bad. The name comes from their illustration: a person who walks any trip under a mile and cycles anything longer arrives sooner from the far destination than the near one. The region beyond the threshold — region beta — is the one that gets treated.

## Mechanism

Repair is triggered by a threshold, not by a running total. Distress above the threshold recruits the responses that end it: people rationalize, leave, complain, seek treatment. Distress below it recruits nothing, because nothing about it demands action today. So the mild version, which is easier to fix, is the one that lasts — the total harm can exceed the severe case simply by not qualifying for attention.
